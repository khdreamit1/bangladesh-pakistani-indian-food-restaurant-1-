Our restaurant

Bangladesh Pakistani Indian Food is a restaurant in Al Olaya, Riyadh, serving Bangladesh, Pakistani and Indian food.

The menu covers fish, meat and chicken, biryani, breads and snacks, drinks and desserts, with prices that stay affordable at SAR 1 to SAR 20 per person.

You can dine in, use the drive-through, or order no-contact delivery.
